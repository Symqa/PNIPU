Second page:

1.The "Stolitsa Zakatov" (Capital of Sunsets) Festival is a large summer music festival in Nizhny Novgorod that runs throughout the summer, from late May through August, at various venues across the city. It features performances by popular artists (rock, pop, indie, and electronic) and creates a unique atmosphere against the backdrop of beautiful sunsets over the Volga and Oka rivers.


2. Nizhny Novgorod is 1,074 kilometers from Perm, approximately 13 hours by car or train. The city itself is located in European Russia, southwest of Perm.

3. Nizhny Novgorod has two main rivers: the Oka and the Volga. The city itself and its main landmark, the Kremlin, are located right where the Oka flows into the Volga. 
The forests of Nizhny Novgorod and the surrounding region are mixed and broadleaf forests with oak, linden, spruce, and pine trees, home to elk, wild boar, and foxes. Within the city limits, popular parks for walks include Switzerland, Sormovsky, and Shchelokovsky Khutor, while the surrounding region boasts extensive forested areas with a rich wildlife ecosystem.
